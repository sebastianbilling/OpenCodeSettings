Designing with the AI

Your cheat sheet for trying ideas in the real app. Nothing you do here reaches the team's code, and nothing is lost.

Every day

  1. Double-click "Start app" on your desktop. A black window opens and the app appears in your browser. Leave the black window open while you work; closing it stops the app.
  2. Open OpenCode and pick the project. Every chat starts with your design partner, the AI.
  3. Describe what you want to see. The AI changes the app and tells you which page to look at. The browser updates by itself; press F5 if it doesn't.
  4. Like it? Save it. Type /save-version or just say "save this". Saved versions can always be opened again.

Four words to know

IdeaA design direction you work on, like "new checkout".
VersionA saved moment of an idea: version 1, 2, 3 and so on.
Unsaved changesEverything changed since the version you have open.
The team's codeThe developers' newest app, without your ideas.

Getting good results

  • Be specific. Say where, what and how much: "On the order page, make the Buy button 48 px tall, colour #1A73E8, with 16 px space above it."
  • One change at a time. Small steps are easier to judge and to undo.
  • Point at what exists. "Use the same card style as on the dashboard."
  • The AI can't see pictures. Describe a mockup in words: layout from top to bottom, sizes, colours as hex codes, fonts.
  • One chat per idea. Start a new chat (New session, or /new) when you switch ideas or when answers get slow.
  • Save when you like something, even small steps. Versions cost nothing.

Good to know

  • Nothing is lost. Before anything is thrown away, the AI keeps a backup. Ask "find what I threw away yesterday" and it can bring it back.
  • Your versions stay on this computer. They are never sent to the team. To show a concept, invite a developer to your screen; the AI can write a short summary of what changed.
  • Old versions stay the same. Getting the team's newest code never changes them.
  • /undo is for design changes only. Use it right after a change you don't like, not after saving, opening a version or getting the latest.
  • When the AI asks you something, it shows buttons. Click the answer that fits, or type your own.
  • Stuck? Type /fix-problems. If that doesn't help, ask Sebastian. There is no rush: your work is safe.
